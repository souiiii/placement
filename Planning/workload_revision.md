## Main corrections and residual uncertainty

- **Technical/core:** targeted retrieval and applied checks replace long linear passes; no separate blanket 30-minute closure allocation. DBMS and backend closure occurs within the existing SQL/MongoDB review budgets, after practice.
- **DSA/practice:** the resource pools are now known, but fluency and exact representative selections are not. DSA is re-estimated at 420 minutes, JavaScript 120, SQL 105 and MongoDB 90; all remain low confidence. Indicative ranges are respectively 300–540, 90–180, 75–150 and 60–150 minutes, not new allocations or automatic extensions.
- **Projects:** each is a project the user built. Velvet and Eventually receive 35-minute refreshes plus separate 45-minute questionnaires; Relay gets 30 + 40. All six sessions and their ordering remain.
- **CV:** 480 → 180 minutes replaces an unsupported large reserve with a smaller working provision. Reliable scope is still absent: 120–360 minutes is an illustrative uncertainty range, not a syllabus estimate or guarantee that three hours suffices.
- **EPQ/RecSys:** EPQ uses a single 60-minute theory refresh plus 90 minutes of numerical/cross-topic work. RecSys unit recall is embedded; final revision is only incremental synthesis/error checking. Separate numericals remains conditional at 45 minutes for uncovered methods only; no semantic overlap comparison or redundancy finding was made.
- **Other provisional items:** RecSys Units 1–3 remain low confidence (first-pass status unconfirmed; indicative 120–210 minutes each). Separate numericals (0–90 depending on incremental need), OA Task 7 (missing original options) and aptitude (unconfirmed assessment scope) remain low confidence. OA Tasks 1/3 retain medium-confidence source-fidelity caveats. Company/HR/IWT no longer have a pending-resource-selection flag because their sources are already chosen.
- **OA/mock:** all four Major and three Minor tasks remain. Previously seen task logic needs refresh and a substantive Java attempt/check, not repeated full reconstruction; the smaller fixes are correspondingly shorter. The full 120-minute mixed mock is unchanged, with a 15-minute debrief.

These estimates presume the stated familiarity. Material that proves unfamiliar requires a focused review of its estimate, not an automatic expansion or deletion of another track. No assumption that missing CV material means no work has been made.
